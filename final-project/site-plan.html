Website Planning Document

Site Name

Mobile Electronic Store

I chose this name because it clearly indicates the primary focus of the website, which is to offer a variety of electronics store in mobile way not just fixed physical shop but shop you can go with any where you go just having your browser you can buy where ever you are. so that why this is Mobile Electonics Store.

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide a comprehensive platform for users to explore and purchase a wide range of electronic devices from various brands. The site aims to offer fast and good services to our customers who are looking for electronics products while they still save their time and money as we deliver them to their doorsteps. And also help those who want to sell their products online to reach a wider audience.

Scenarios

  • How can I find and compare different phones and laptops?
  • How do I add products to my cart and complete a purchase?
  • Where can I see the total price of my selected items before checkout?

Color Schema

The website uses a simple and professional color scheme:

  • Primary Blue (#006EB6): Used for headers, footer, navigation bars, buttons, and important headings.
  • Deep Teal Blue (#0c677e): Used for body text, borders.
  • Light Gray (#f5f5f5): Used as the main page background.

Typography

  • Barlow Condensed: Used for headings and body text to create a modern, clean appearance.

This font was selected because it is easy to read and works well on both mobile and desktop screens.
